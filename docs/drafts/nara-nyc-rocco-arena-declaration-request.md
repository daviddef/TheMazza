## Notes for David (not part of the letter)

- The index line gives no age, birthplace or address, so it is a CANDIDATE for the Rocco Arena of this family, not a proved one. The same search also returned a different Rocco Arena declaration of 1927 (record 245624), which is not attached.
- The family fits: in New York from May 1892, a ship arrival in 1894 is a candidate, and the 1900 manifest of the Trave calls him a U.S. citizen.
- No petition by a Rocco Arena was found in the free index for 1880-1930, but that index does not cover the New York City state and city courts.
