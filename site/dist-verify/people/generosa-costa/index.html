Where this comes from

All 4 of the relationships drawn above come from Michael Mazza's family tree, and none of them has been proved by a record this archive has read.

7 records have been read about them:

  • Scilla, Nati 1893, atto 22 — named as the mother, a filatrice — birth register
  • Ellis Island — sought among the Costa of Scilla, not found — passenger manifest
  • United States Census 1900, Manhattan Ward 6 — aged 30, married ten years — census
  • Scilla, Matrimoni 1888, atto 63 — documented addition
  • Scilla, Nati 1893, atto 22 — documented
  • Antenati name index, Scilla, atto di morte 6 June 1916 — documented
  • Antenati name index, Scilla — atti di morte 6 June 1916 and 20 April 1937 — certain
What a register says about this person, which the tree does not.

Four Costa gave Scilla as their last residence at Ellis Island and none is Generosa. Her crossing, which the tree dates to 1894, is not in the index under that name.

Indexed as 'Genneroso Arena'. The census makes her thirty in 1900 and married ten years, so born about 1870 and married in 1890. The New York City birth register gives her four more children than the tree has.

ROCCO ARENA'S WIFE, and SHE IS IN THE FAMILY TREE -- this row exists only to connect her to parents the tree does not have. Her marriage act of December 1888 reads «COSTA GENEROSA, FILATRICE, nata in Scilla, FIGLIA DI ANTONIO, residente in Scilla, E DI ANNA ARLOTTA, residente in Scilla». Both parents were recorded in this archive as documented people and NEITHER WAS CONNECTED TO HER, so no chart and no page could show that Rocco Arena's wife had known parents. That is the same failure as the five Melluso who sat in a spreadsheet column: a fact held in one file does not reach the reader until it is joined to another.

Trade -- missing entirely — FILATRICE, a spinner.

Death -- missing entirely — DIED AT SCILLA ON 6 JUNE 1916, «Padre ANTONINO COSTA, Madre ANNA ARLOTTA, Coniuge ROCCO ARENA». Her parents are exactly those the marriage act of 1888 gave, now from a second and independent document, and her husband is named. SHE DIED SIXTEEN YEARS BEFORE HIM: Rocco Arena followed her on 8 February 1932.

Two women of one name — A GUARD, NOT A FINDING — THERE ARE TWO AND THEY MUST NEVER BE MERGED. (1) GENEROSA COSTA, born about 1868, a filatrice, daughter of ANTONINO COSTA and ANNA ARLOTTA, married ROCCO ARENA at Scilla in December 1888, died at Scilla 6 JUNE 1916. She is on this archive's spine -- Anna Arena's mother. (2) A second GENEROSA COSTA, wife of PASQUALE DONATO, who died 20 April 1937; Pasquale is a son of DOMENICO DONATO and NUNZIATA SOFI, which makes him MICHELE DONATO'S BROTHER. So the two women sit one in each of this archive's two Scilla lines, the Arena and the Donato, and a tree that meets both will be tempted to fuse them. THE SEPARATION IS EVIDENCED, not assumed: the first was already dead in 1916, twenty-one years before the second's husband died, and their parents differ. COSTA AT SCILLA IS NOT A SMALL NAME -- the name index holds seventy-six Costa records and eighteen Costa deaths, including a THIRD Generosa in the previous generation, GENEROSA POLISTENA, for whom Rocco's wife was very likely named.

Where the two disagree, the chart above still draws the tree as it stands. This archive does not silently rewrite what it was given — the acts are here.

The other archives

The Mazza Archive is one of seven, each following a different line of the same family. They use the same method and the same five words for how sure a thing is.

  • The Defranceschi Archive Istria · Kvarner · Carnia A family written down under eight sovereigns, in four languages, and spelled at least nine different ways. Defranceschi · De Franceschi · Defranceski · Defrančeski
  • The Falco Archive Arienzo, near Naples Nine documented generations born, married and buried in one valley, and then a migrant ship to Brisbane. Falco · Zampiello · Morgillo · Crisci · Liguori · Maione · Rivetti · Annecchino
  • The Blažević Archive Senj and the Krmpotska mountains An evidence-first archive for the family of Hedviga Blažević of Senj — carriers, a tobacco factory, and a wall grave. Blažević · Papić · Vukelić · Žubrinić · Kalanj
  • The Booyzen Archive Graaff-Reinet · Cradock · Johannesburg A Cape Dutch family that kept marrying British women, changed one letter of its name in a war, and came off the train at Johannesburg with nothing. Booyzen · Booysen · Kolbe · Barry · Downing · van Jaarsveld · Schoeman
  • The Lerena Archive Rosario · the Cape · the Rand A horse-dealer out of Argentina, a stable under Table Mountain, and four family traditions tested against the record rather than repeated. Lerena · Taylor
  • The D'Arcy Archive England and Ireland · Parramatta · Brisbane Out with the 39th Regiment in 1825, and four generations afterwards in two Brisbane cemeteries. D'Arcy · Saniger · Sneyd · Hornby · Wakefield · Atkinson · Murdoch
