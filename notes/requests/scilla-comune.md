# One request to the Comune di Scilla — two acts inside the closed period

**Why these two and no others.** Antenati publishes the Scilla civil registers generously — marriages
**1866–1913** (48 volumes), banns **1866–1913** (46), deaths **1866–1943** (81), births to 1904. Two
acts this archive wants fall *outside* all of that, in the period still held by the comune. Everything
else has been, or can be, read online.

---

## 1. Marriage — GIOVANNI POLISTENA × NUNZIATA DONATO, about 1916

The marriage series on Antenati stops at **1913**, and 1913 has been read in full — all 58 marriages
of that year — and does not contain them. Giovanni Polistena sailed to New Orleans in 1908 and was
back at Scilla to marry; their eldest recorded child dates the marriage to about **1916**.

**What it would give.** Both spouses' ages, trades and parents; and Giovanni Polistena's own parents,
where this archive currently has only the 1908 manifest's statement.

## 2. Death — NUNZIATA DONATO, 1975, act no. 30

Wife of Giovanni Polistena. Daughter of **MICHELE DONATO** (born about 1856, of Via Grotte, Scilla)
and **MARIA SOFI**, *filatrice*.

**What it would give.** Her date and place of birth — and, in the parents' names, one more independent
statement of **Michele Donato's** identity. His *comune of birth* is the single fact this archive has
failed to find in three complete sweeps of the Scilla birth registers (1851, 1856, 1859) and five
years of marriage indexes (1874–1878).

## The letter (Italian)

> Spett.le Ufficio di Stato Civile
> Comune di Scilla (RC)
>
> Oggetto: **richiesta di estratti per riassunto — atti di matrimonio e di morte, a fini di ricerca genealogica**
>
> Il sottoscritto, discendente della famiglia, chiede il rilascio di **estratti per riassunto** dei
> seguenti atti:
>
> 1. **Atto di matrimonio** fra **POLISTENA GIOVANNI** e **DONATO NUNZIATA**, celebrato in codesto
>    Comune **presumibilmente negli anni 1915–1918**;
> 2. **Atto di morte n. 30 dell'anno 1975**, intestato a **DONATO NUNZIATA**, coniugata **POLISTENA
>    GIOVANNI**, figlia di **DONATO MICHELE** e di **SOFI MARIA**.
>
> La richiesta è avanzata **esclusivamente a fini di ricerca genealogica familiare**.
>
> Ringraziando anticipatamente, porgo distinti saluti.
>
> [nome, indirizzo, recapito, data]

**How to send it.** To the comune's *Ufficio di Stato Civile*, by PEC or ordinary email; Antenati's
own **"Richieste di certificati"** page lists the route. Genealogical requests are normally free, and
an *estratto per riassunto* is enough — a *copia integrale* sometimes requires a stated legal interest.

**While waiting, the free alternative for Michele Donato** is his **death act**. The Scilla death
series runs to **1943** and its annual index carries a patronymic as well as a name. A death act names
the birthplace outright. That sweep is open and unfinished.
