# Request to the Comune di Scilla — death act of Nunziata Donato, 1975

**Why it cannot be found online.** Antenati publishes Italian civil registers only after the
statutory closure period. Scilla's series on the portal stop at 1913 for marriages and 1904 for
births; a 1975 death is far inside the closed period and is held by the **comune**, not the
Archivio di Stato. It has to be asked for.

**What is known, so that the clerk does not have to search.** Nunziata Donato, wife of Giovanni
Polistena, died at Scilla in **1975**, act **number 30**. Her father was **Michele Donato** (born
about 1856, a Scilla man, of Via Grotte) and her mother **Maria Sofi**, *filatrice*.

**What the act would add.** An Italian death act of this period gives the exact date and place of
death, the deceased's date and place of birth, her last residence, her occupation, her parents by
name, her husband by name, and the declarant. It would date Nunziata to the day at both ends, and
it would give **Michele Donato's comune of birth** — the single fact this archive has failed to
find in three complete sweeps of the Scilla birth registers.

## The letter (Italian)

> Spett.le Ufficio di Stato Civile
> Comune di Scilla (RC)
>
> Oggetto: **richiesta di estratto per riassunto dell'atto di morte — DONATO NUNZIATA, anno 1975, atto n. 30**
>
> Il sottoscritto, discendente della famiglia, chiede il rilascio di un **estratto per riassunto**
> (o copia integrale, se possibile) dell'**atto di morte n. 30 dell'anno 1975**, intestato a
> **DONATO NUNZIATA**, coniugata **POLISTENA GIOVANNI**, figlia di **DONATO MICHELE** e di
> **SOFI MARIA**.
>
> La richiesta è avanzata a **fini di ricerca genealogica familiare**.
>
> Ringraziando anticipatamente, porgo distinti saluti.
>
> [nome, indirizzo, recapito, data]

**Where to send it.** The comune's *Ufficio di Stato Civile*, by PEC or ordinary email; Antenati's
own **"Richieste di certificati"** page lists the route. Requests for genealogical purposes are
normally free of charge, and an *estratto per riassunto* is enough — the *copia integrale* sometimes
needs a stated legal interest.

**One useful alternative, now that the coverage note has been corrected.** Michele Donato's own
**marriage** is about 1875–77, and Antenati's Scilla marriage series runs **1866 to 1913** without a
gap. A marriage act names the groom's comune of birth outright. That is free, immediate, and does
the same job — and it has not yet been searched.
